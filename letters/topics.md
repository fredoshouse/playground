## Rules for this list

1. **A topic earns its place by being said twice.** If it keeps coming back in the
   check-ins, it's real. If it was said once and never again, it was a mood.
2. **Seeds keep his words.** Cleaned up, not rewritten. The voice guide is
   `thirty/voice/VOICE.md`.
3. **Private stays private.** `the-base.md` has his savings rate, his debt, and his
   marriage in it. It is a letter only if he decides it is — and probably a different,
   smaller version of itself. Nothing publishes without him saying so.
4. **One letter a week is the target.** Not three. One that lands.
