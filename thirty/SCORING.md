# Scoring

## Daily points — 17 possible, 15 on a non-run day

| Key | What | Pts |
|-----|------|-----|
| `move` | Workout done | 3 |
| `run` | 3 miles run, or 30 min incline walk | +2 |
| `home` | Ate at home all day, no takeout | 2 |
| `write` | 20 min / 500 words | 3 |
| `photo` | Photo taken + one line logged | 1 |
| `pray` | 10+ min | 2 |
| `phone` | Socials under 60 min | 2 |
| `sleep` | In bed 10:30, phone out of the room | 2 |

**Day grades:** A ≥ 13 · B 11–12 · C 9–10 · D < 9.

## The consequence — pushup debt
Every miss on a day you *intended* to hit adds to the debt. Debt is real, it carries,
and it gets paid.

| Miss | Pushups |
|------|---------|
| Scheduled workout skipped | 25 |
| Ate out (beyond the weekly allowance) | 30 |
| No writing | 20 |
| Over the social limit | 15 |
| Blew bedtime | 15 |
| No photo | 10 |
| No prayer | 20 |

**Rules of the debt**
1. Pay it within 48 hours or it **doubles.**
2. Debt is capped at 100/day owed so it can't spiral into a reason to quit — but the
   cap doesn't erase it, it queues it.
3. Sunday night, whatever's left gets paid before the week closes. No rollover into a
   new week. You start every Monday at zero.

## The unlock — this is the actual point
The reward isn't a treat, it's **access.** Things I enjoy get locked behind the week.

| Week total | What unlocks |
|------------|--------------|
| **≥ 90** | Full weekend: night out, one discretionary purchase, gaming/TV without guilt |
| **75–89** | One of the three. Pick it deliberately. |
| **60–74** | Nothing discretionary. Rest, but earn it back. |
| **< 60** | No discretionary spend, no night out, and the week's Big Rock moves to the top of next week. |

The whole design: make the cheap dopamine expensive and the good stuff earned, so it
tastes like something again.

## Backfill — the 48-hour window
A day you never logged scores zero and owes the full cap, same as a day you blew. But
they aren't the same thing, and the system says so: an unlogged day reads **not logged**,
not D.

You can backfill it for **48 hours.** Tell me what actually happened on that day and it
gets scored for real, and the debt recalculates. After 48 hours it locks at zero and
stays there.

This is the one piece of grace in the system, and it's deliberately narrow. It exists
because "I did the work but didn't check in" and "I did nothing" shouldn't cost the same
— but only if you say so while you still remember the day.

## Streaks tracked

- **Move streak** — days since a full skip.
