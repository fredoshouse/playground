# The 30 — Sept 6 → Oct 5, 2026

**One sentence:** For thirty days I run my life like I ran football — a plan, a score, a
consequence — so that when October gets here I'm the guy I said I'd be, not the guy who
meant to be.

## Why now
- I just turned 30. She just turned 30. That season is over; this one starts on purpose.
- I've lost the feeling of *enjoying* things because everything is always available.
  The fix isn't more discipline for its own sake — it's putting friction back in front
  of the cheap stuff so the good stuff lands again.
- September is already loaded: anniversary, wedding planning, marriage license,
  counseling, the yard sale, painting the house, the conference. This system is how all
  of that gets carried at once instead of one thing at a time.

## The seven dailies
These are the only things scored. Everything else is a Big Rock (see below).

| # | Daily | Standard |
|---|-------|----------|
| 1 | **Move** | A real workout. 4–5x/week. |
| 2 | **Run** | 2x/week minimum, 3 miles. Incline walk (30 min) counts when the legs are cooked. |
| 3 | **Eat home** | No eating out. Floor is 3 days/week, target is 5. Streak-tracked. |
| 4 | **Write** | 20 minutes or 500 words, whatever comes first. Feeds the site — 1 published article/week. |
| 5 | **Capture** | One photo + one line about the day. Thirty days = one story, not thirty posts. |
| 6 | **Pray** | 10 minutes minimum. Not a checkbox — the anchor. |
| 7 | **Off the phone** | Socials under 60 min. In bed by 10:30, phone out of the room. |

## Big Rocks — September
One per week, dated, not floating.
- [ ] Painting + photos hung (this weekend)
- [ ] Marriage license
- [ ] Book marriage counseling
- [ ] Anniversary planned and paid for
- [ ] Yard sale run
- [ ] Website: 4 articles live by Oct 5
- [ ] Conference prep — October readiness
- [ ] Finish the book I'm in the middle of
- [ ] Money: no discretionary spend that isn't equipment that earns

## Money rule
Two categories only: **debt down** and **equipment that earns.** Anything else waits
for the 30 to end. Eating out is the leak — that's why it's a scored daily and not a
suggestion.

## The point
Not to be perfect. To be *countable.* At the end of thirty days I want a number and a
folder of photos that prove I showed up, and a body and a marriage and a bank account
that felt it.
