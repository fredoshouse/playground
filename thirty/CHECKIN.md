# Check-ins

## Daily — evening, 5 questions, under two minutes
Answer in one line each. I fill the log, do the math, tell you the damage.

1. **Did you move today — and was it a run?**
2. **Did you eat at home, all day?**
3. **Did you write, and did you take the photo? What's the line for today?**
4. **Prayer, phone, and what time are you in bed?**
5. **What's the one thing tomorrow that would make it a good day?**

Then you get back: today's points, the grade, your streaks, and what you owe.

## Morning — 3 framing questions
1. **What's the one thing today that actually moves something?**
2. **What's the thing you'll be tempted to skip?** (Name it now — that's the whole trick.)
3. **What are you grateful for, and what are you asking for?**

## Weekly — Sunday night
1. Score, grade, streaks — what does the number say?
2. Which Big Rock moved, and which one didn't? Why not?
3. Debt: clear it. Log it.
4. What did I enjoy this week — and did it land, or was I numb through it?
5. Did I lead well this week? Her, the house, the money, my walk with God.
6. Set next week's Big Rock and put the workouts on the calendar before Monday.

## The rules of the check-in
- Honest beats good. A logged D is worth more than an unlogged A.
- Missing a check-in is itself a 15-pushup miss. The log is the system.
- If you're traveling or with friends, say so in the check-in and we adjust the day's
  target instead of pretending — an adjusted day is not a missed day.
