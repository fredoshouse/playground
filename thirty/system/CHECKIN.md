# Check-in

One email a night, 9:30 PM. Three questions.

1. **Did you move?**
2. **Did you eat at home?**
3. **What time are you in bed?**

"Yes, yes, 11" is a complete answer. Anything you want to add goes in the log as the
day's line, but it's optional.

You get back: today's points, your streaks, and anything you owe.

## Weekly — Sunday night
Weeks run Sunday to Saturday, so Sunday night reviews the week that just ended and sets
the one starting Monday.

1. What does the number say?
2. Which Big Rock moved, and which didn't?
3. Clear the debt. It doesn't carry into Monday.
4. Did I lead well — her, the house, the money, my walk?

## The rules
- Honest beats good. A logged D is worth more than an unlogged A.
- Travelling or out with people? Say so and the day gets adjusted, not counted as a miss.
- Nothing gets added to the three until seven days are logged in a row.
