# Scoring

## Daily — 7 possible
| Key | What | Pts |
|-----|------|-----|
| `move` | Workout, run, or 30 min incline walk | 3 |
| `home` | Ate at home all day | 2 |
| `sleep` | In bed by 10:30, phone out of the room | 2 |

**Grades:** A = 7 (all three) · B = 5–6 · C = 3–4 · D under 3.

## Weekly — 49 possible
| Target | Floor |
|--------|-------|
| Moved | 4 days |
| Ate home | 3 days |
| In bed on time | 5 days |

## Pushup debt
| Miss | Pushups |
|------|---------|
| Didn't move | 25 |
| Ate out | 30 |
| Blew bedtime | 15 |

Pay within 48 hours or it doubles. Capped at 100 a day and **300 total** — a number you
can't pay isn't a consequence, it's an excuse to quit. Sunday night it clears; you start
every week at zero.

## Three states, not two
`- [x]` done. `- [ ]` missed. `- [?]` asked but not answered.

Unknown is not the same as missed. A `?` scores no points and costs no pushups, and the
day's total drops to match — answer three of three and you get an A even if only two were
asked. The system never charges for something it doesn't know.

## Backfill — 48 hours
An unlogged day reads **not logged**, not D, and can be backfilled for 48 hours: say
what actually happened and it scores for real. After that it locks at zero.

## The unlock
| Week total | What unlocks |
|------------|--------------|
| **44+** | Full weekend — night out, one purchase, screens without guilt |
| **37–43** | One of the three. Pick it deliberately. |
| **29–36** | Nothing discretionary. Rest, then earn it back. |
| **under 29** | No spend, no night out. The week's Big Rock goes to the top of next week. |

Make the cheap dopamine expensive and the good stuff earned, so it tastes like
something again.
