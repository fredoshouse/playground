# The 30 — through Oct 5, 2026

**One sentence:** Three things a day, every day, until October — so that when the
conference and the wedding and the rest of it arrive, I'm the guy I said I'd be.

## What happened to version one
It opened with seven scored dailies, two emails a day, and a pushup debt that could
reach four figures. Three days in, nothing had been logged. The design was too big to
start from zero, so it got cut down. Days 1–3 are void — no score, no debt, nothing
owed. That version isn't a failure to carry around; it was a bad first draft.

## The three
| # | Daily | Standard | Pts |
|---|-------|----------|-----|
| 1 | **Move** | A real workout, a run, or a 30-minute incline walk | 3 |
| 2 | **Eat home** | No takeout, no eating out | 2 |
| 3 | **Sleep** | In bed by 10:30, phone out of the room | 2 |

Seven a day. Forty-nine a week. That's the whole system.

These three were picked because they carry the most: the body, the money, and the thing
that makes every other habit possible. Nothing gets added until there's a streak worth
protecting.

## What comes back, and when

After **fourteen**, add the daily photo and the line that goes with it.
Prayer and the phone limit were never really scoreable. They're better as things I do
than things I count.
